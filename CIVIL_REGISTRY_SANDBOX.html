🔄 System Lifecycle Flow

1
Verification (POST /verify)
Compares entered BSN with Civil Registry records. Rejects deceased files.
2
Registration Write-Back (POST /register)
Upon approval, registers profile and flags BSN mapping as issued in the DB.
3
Lifecycle trigger (POST /lifecycle-event)
Death notification locks the citizen eID profile and revokes credentials.
