1. Account Application & Registration Wizard

To register your national eID profile, navigate to the eID Portal homepage and click the **Register** link to launch the 4-step onboarding wizard.

Step 01
Identity & Demographics

Enter your authoritative personal info. You must provide your **9-digit CRIB / Citizen ID Number** (assigned by the Sint Maarten Tax Authority). Ensure your spelling matches your passport.

Step 02
Residence & District Selection

Specify your local residential address. Select your district/municipality (e.g. *Philipsburg, Simpson Bay, Cul de Sac, Cole Bay*) from the drop-down menu to map municipal permissions.

Step 03
Security & MFA setup

Input your email address and active mobile phone number. Choose your preferred **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** fallback option: Google Authenticator (TOTP), SMS OTP, or Passkey/FIDO2 biometrics.

Step 04
Liveness Check & Document Upload

Upload a high-resolution scan of your passport or ID card. You can select **Capture with Webcam** to perform a live selfie liveness check, or choose **Select Photo File** to upload a portrait image directly.

2. Logging In & Accessing Government Portals

Once registered and activated by the Civil Registry, you can use your eID to sign in to any federated government portal (such as the *MyGov Portal* or *Tax Authority Portal*).

Phase A
OIDC Federated Login Redirect

When you click "Login with eID" on an agency portal, you are redirected to the secure central WSO2 login gate. Enter your UIN, Email address, or eID Serial alongside your password.

Phase B
Multi-Factor Authentication Challenge

Depending on the security tier of the portal (e.g., Tax and Health require High Assurance), the portal will challenge you to input a **6-digit verification code** generated by your Google Authenticator app or sent via SMS/Email.

Phase C
Scope Consent Approval

Review the metadata consent prompt. WSO2 will display the specific attributes requested by the portal (e.g. *Email, Name, UIN, CRIB*). Check the approval boxes to permit the attribute release.

3. Google Authenticator (TOTP) & SMS Mobile OTP

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) protects your identity from unauthorized access. The eID portal supports two dynamic second-factor options:

📱 Google Authenticator (TOTP)

Using a Time-Based One-Time Password (TOTP) app is the most secure offline option.

  • QR Setup Toggle: The QR enrollment canvas is hidden behind an expandable trigger link to prevent shoulder surfing or scanning by unauthorized onlookers.
  • Key Backup: Save the printed alphanumeric secret seed in a secure location to restore access if you change or lose your mobile device.
  • Verification Check: You must enter a valid 6-digit code from your app to complete configuration and link the device.
💬 SMS Mobile OTP (BulkSMSBD Integration)

SMS OTP delivers one-time security codes directly to your mobile phone number.

  • 60-Second Cooldown: When you request an SMS OTP, a countdown timer starts. You cannot request a new SMS code until the 60-second limit expires.
  • 6-Digit Format Guard: The input form strictly validates that the code you type is exactly 6 numeric digits before transmitting it. Non-numeric characters are blocked automatically.

⚠️ Security Compliance Warning

To comply with international NIST guidelines, SMS OTP is vulnerable to SIM-swapping exploits. We highly recommend registering a **Touch ID / Face ID Passkey** or using **Google Authenticator (TOTP)** as your primary MFA method for sensitive portals.

4. Cryptographic Passkeys & The Last Factor Guard

eID Passkeys use public-key cryptography (FIDO2 / WebAuthn standards) to allow secure, passwordless logins using biometric scanners (Touch ID / Face ID) or physical hardware keys (YubiKeys).

🔑 FIDO2 Biometric Enrollment

From the **Security Vault** on your dashboard, click **Register Passkey** to prompt your device's biometric scanner. Your private key remains locked on your device, and only the public key is registered with the eID database.

🛡️ eIDAS Last Factor Guard (Strict Lockout Prevention)

Under eIDAS Level High compliance, your profile **must always have at least one active MFA factor enabled**.

🔒 Security Rule

The dashboard will **block** you from revoking/deleting your last remaining MFA method. If you try to delete your only registered passkey, the system will trigger a validation error:

"eIDAS Assurance Level High requires at least one active Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) method. Please configure Google Authenticator or SMS verification before revoking your last remaining passkey."

To revoke your last passkey, you must first set up Google Authenticator or configure verified SMS delivery as your alternative factor.

5. OIDC Single Logout (SLO) Protection

To protect your privacy when accessing government portals on shared or public devices (e.g., library computers, internet cafes), the eID system implements a secure multi-service Single Logout (SLO) protocol.

🔄 The Single Logout Chain

Clicking "Logout" on the MyGov portal (`mygov.test`) triggers an automatic, secure redirect loop that cleanses all sessions:

  • Laravel Session: Clears your active session on the relying agency portal.
  • eID Portal Session: The browser is redirected to `http://localhost:3000/logout` to clear the local browser storage keys (`eid_token`, `eid_profile`).
  • WSO2 Broker Session: Contacts WSO2 Carbon to invalidate your active SSO identity token.
  • eID Backend Session: Contacts the eID API to revoke your server-side session token.

🔒 Shared Device Guideline

Always wait for the logout redirects to complete fully and display the portal login screen before leaving your browser. This ensures that no cached session tokens remain in the browser local storage.

6. Annex E Cryptographic Transaction Auditing

The eID portal provides complete accountability and transparency regarding how your data is accessed by government agencies.

📋 Evidence Records

Every time an agency requests your details, a non-repudiation record is written to the eID audit ledger. You can inspect these logs under the **Evidence Audit Logs** tab on your dashboard:

  • Reference ID: The unique transaction token generated for the authentication request.
  • Subject UIN: Your unique national citizen identifier.
  • Verification Status: The level of assurance verification used (e.g., `VERIFIED_HIGH_ASSURANCE`).
  • Payload Hash: A cryptographic SHA-256 hash of the attributes shared with the agency, guaranteeing that the data has not been modified.
