1. Running Assisted Onboarding

When a citizen approaches the counter for eID enrollment, open the **PSC Assisted** tab (`/admin/psc-assisted`) to initiate the enrollment wizard.

Action 01
Lookup Citizen Profile

Enter the applicant's UIN, Email address, or Full Name in the search console. Click **Search Citizen**. Verify their identity photo and demographics.

Action 02
Execute Biometric Face Capture

Instruct the citizen to face the camera. Click **Start Capture Camera** to activate the webcam stream overlay. Align their head inside the blue dotted guide line and click **Snap Photo**. Ensure the match validation score exceeds **95%** threshold to comply with eIDAS High assurance mandates.

Action 03
Document Inspection

Inspect the citizen's physical passport or ID card. Check the security features under UV magnifying light. Once checked, check the **Documents Inspected** box to approve.

Action 04
Commit & Generate eID

Click **Commit Enrollment & Issue eID**. The backend database will activate their profile and automatically output their official eID serial key.

🚨 Physical Verification Standard

Operators must strictly verify that the individual sitting at the service window matches the photograph in the physical passport. Assisted approvals without a face-to-face inspection are severe compliance violations.

2. ePassport NFC & MRZ Scan Mappings

For passports supporting electronic chips, execute automated verification on the **ePassport Scan** page (`/admin/epassport-stub`).

Pattern A
Automated NFC Chip Verification

Place the passport face down on the scanner surface. Click **Trigger ePassport Scan**. The scanner reads the MRZ, calculates the BAC key, and validates the contactless chip.

  • Passed: Displays `CSCA_CHIP_VERIFIED_PASS`. Proceed with biometric enroller.
  • Failed: Initiates Pattern B fallback automatically.
Pattern B
Documentary Manual Fallback

If the chip is corrupted or the passport is non-electronic, enter details manually:

  • Input the physical Passport Number and Expiration Date.
  • Confirm visual inspection of the physical holograms/watermarks.
  • Click **Commit Pattern B Verification** to save.

3. Outage Protocols: Offline Check Point Scans

During system network outages, operators switch to the **Offline Verify** scanner portal (`/admin/offline-verification`).

Scanning Offline eID QR Codes

1. Ask the citizen to display their eID Offline QR code from their mobile dashboard app.
2. Scan the QR code or paste the token payload into the textarea. Click **Verify Offline Token**.
3. The system executes the **RS256 digital signature validation** locally using the public key. If the certificate is authentic and has not expired, it parses and displays the citizen's verified details.

🔒 Offline Signature Cryptography

Offline verification relies entirely on verifying signatures against the central registry's certificate. Since the portal is disconnected from the central server, operators must verify the "Certificate Validity" date displayed on the output layout.
